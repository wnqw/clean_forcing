## Video Comparison Study — Instructions

In this study you will see **15 pairs of videos**. In each pair, the two
videos — labeled **A** (left) and **B** (right) — were generated by computer
programs from the **same text prompt**. The prompt is shown above the video.

For each pair:

1. **Watch the whole clip at least once, paying attention to both sides,
   before answering.** Each clip is a 10-second excerpt.
Choose the video you genuinely think is better in each respect. **There are
no right or wrong answers** — except where one video has obvious quality
problems. Go with your overall impression; do not overthink any single
question.

Please keep your attention on the videos throughout. Obviously careless
responses (e.g., answering without playing the clip) may not be approved.

The session takes about 12–15 minutes. Thank you for participating!
